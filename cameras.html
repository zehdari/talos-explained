The vehicle · Chapter 8

The cameras

Two stereo cameras, one looking ahead and one looking down, are Talos's main way to find the props. This chapter follows their pixels from the glass to a ROS topic, the detector and your laptop.

The short version
  • Two Stereolabs ZED X Mini stereo cameras, ffc (forward) and dfc (down), on GMSL2 to the Jetson Orin.
  • The ZED SDK grabs 1920 × 1200 at 15 Hz, rectifies with the underwater calibration and computes depth on the GPU. zed.launch.py runs one ZED node per camera.
  • The detector reads one camera at a time; behaviour trees switch it with set_camera_is_dfc.
  • A raw frame is 9.2 MB, 1.1 Gbit/s at 15 Hz. Only JPEGs should cross the tether.
  • Nereus publishes the topics the stack uses, with the same names and frames, so the detector, mapping and trees run unchanged.

Two ZED X Minis

Each ZED X Mini (camera_model: zedxm in ffc_config.yaml and dfc_config.yaml) has two global-shutter sensors behind one window, so one camera tells the software what a prop is and how far away it is.

Two eyes, one depth image

A near point lands at quite different columns in the left and right images, a far one at almost the same column. That shift, the disparity d, gives depth z = fB/d: a prop 2 m away shifts about 46 px, one 4 m away only 23 px, so matching errors cost more depth far away. Two lenses make depth has the maths.

From the lens to a ROS topic

After the capture card it is all software on the Orin.

  • The ZED SDK (Stereolabs' closed-source library, version 5) grabs, rectifies and computes depth. zed-ros2-wrapper wraps it as a ROS 2 component; riptide.repos pulls its master branch, not a submodule.
  • The wrapper only publishes what someone subscribes to, but depth runs on every frame anyway: positional tracking is on (wrapper default) and needs it, so both cameras always pay for depth on the GPU (wrapper isDepthRequired(), in f6978f5). A forgotten image subscription on your laptop still costs Orin time to copy and compress the image, and tether bandwidth.

zed.launch.py and its parameter files

  • zed.launch.py starts one process, zed_container, with two ZedCamera components named zed_node in namespaces ffc and dfc: topics /talos/ffc/zed_node/… and /talos/dfc/zed_node/…. Plus picture_taker.py (forward camera only) for calibration pairs.
  • respawn=False: one crash takes both cameras down until the launch is restarted. The remote launcher starts it from talos_launch_mpc.yaml and monitors both rgb/camera_info topics to show it is up.
  • Three parameter files per camera, later ones win: the wrapper's common_stereo.yaml and zedxm.yaml, then Talos's ffc_config.yaml or dfc_config.yaml. Anything Talos doesn't set follows the wrapper version installed on the Orin.

Not loaded, though they look like camera settings (riptide_hardware/cfg):

  • zed_compression.yaml mostly restricts every ZED image topic to raw transport (d0e61d7, "removed compressed topics"), plus ffmpeg encoder settings. Loaded as it is, it would remove the /compressed topic the detector reads. Its last user, a liltank block, went in b905e3e.
  • zed_common.yaml (an old copy of common_stereo.yaml), tank_ffc_config.yaml (liltank), the USB-camera files and the older calibrations.
  • zed_manager.py pauses one camera's process to save the GPU, but it expects separate ffc_container and dfc_container processes. Both cameras now share one container and nothing launches it.
The calibration path is absolute

optional_opencv_calibration_file points at /home/ros/colcon_deploy/src/riptide_hardware/cfg/… on the Orin, so a new calibration needs a deploy and a restart of zed.launch.py.

What each camera sees

Rectifying crops a little, so the detector's view is 54.5° × 35.7° forward and 54.9° × 36.0° down, against about 59° × 39° raw.

Compression and bandwidth

image_transport gives every raw image topic a /compressed twin holding a JPEG (quality 95, the default). The bench frame above is 537 KB: about 64 Mbit/s at 15 Hz, 17 times less than raw (its trip to your laptop).

  • Raw images don't fit through the tether. One raw stream is 1.1 Gbit/s; simulation_hil.launch.py notes that full-resolution depth "saturates gigabit, so raw topics never arrive".
  • Depth stays on the Orin. JPEG can't carry 32-bit floats, so the viewer's Depth card pulls raw 9.2 MB images: close it during a run. For depth on a laptop, the compressedDepth transport (lossless PNG) is smaller but still large; nothing in the stack uses it and it is unconfirmed on the Orin.
  • picture_taker pulls raw stereo the whole time. It subscribes to the forward camera's side-by-side stereo_raw/image_raw_color from launch (picture_taker.py): 18.4 MB × 15 Hz, about 2.2 Gbit/s between processes on the Orin. It doesn't cross the tether, but the ZED node has to make and send it. Turn it off when you aren't calibrating (command below).
  • Measure one camera topic at a time. Several subscriptions at once add load and skew the rates, so pretest checks each stream for 8 Hz in turn.
  • Video codecs aren't used. H.265 would be smaller (the team forks ffmpeg_image_transport), but zed_compression.yaml picks NVIDIA's hevc_nvenc encoder, not confirmed to work on the Orin; both ends need the plugin; and a lost packet corrupts frames until the next keyframe.

One detector, two cameras

The detector, yolo_orientation, listens to one camera at a time, with its own YOLO model and thresholds per camera (yolo_orientation.yaml):

ffc
rs26_ffc_finals.pt: 11 classes (gate and torpedo-board symbols, torpedo holes, slalom, buoy, magnet), confidence 0.65
dfc
dfc_rs_26.pt: 8 classes (bin and table symbols), confidence 0.8
Torpedo logic
The special fire/blood torpedo-hole handling runs only on torpedo_task_camera (ffc); on the down camera those symbols are plain bin classes

The set_camera_is_dfc service (std_srvs/SetBool, true = down) swaps everything at once:

  1. It replies at once and swaps 0.1 s later (switch_camera_callback): success doesn't mean the new camera is live.
  2. It loads the other model: quick with a prebuilt TensorRT .engine, slow if export: true has to build one. Pretest allows 90 s.
  3. Under a lock, it swaps model, classes, thresholds and frame id together.
  4. It subscribes to the new camera's three topics and detects nothing until the new camera_info arrives.

The same topics from Nereus

In simulation the ZED nodes don't run: the Nereus bridge (bridge.yaml) publishes images rendered from two stereo_camera sensors in the Talos robot pack (robot.yaml).

  • Same names and frames, 15 Hz: rgb/image_rect_color/compressed, depth/depth_registered, rgb/camera_info and point_cloud/cloud_registered (5 Hz, every 8th pixel). No raw colour or stereo_raw.
  • Already rectified, 1920 × 1200, zero distortion; colour only as JPEG (quality 93).
  • Frames: the URDF still places talos/<cam>_camera_link; the bridge adds the optical frames itself (bridge.yaml). So sim works without zed_wrapper, which riptide_setup skips on machines without an NVIDIA driver; navigation.launch.py then prints zed_wrapper not found. Launching without zed TF, which is normal there.
  • Depth with ZED-like flaws: 0.15–4 m on both cameras, noise growing with z^2, dropouts, outliers, and NaN where invalid.

In the simulator, the viewer's camera cards switch between Truth (its own render, no noise or latency) and ROS (what the bridge publishes). On the robot they show the ROS topics.

Keep Nereus's numbers in step

Nereus's down camera still uses dfc_charuco_test.yaml (f = 1857.5 px, robot.yaml); the robot moved to zedxm_dfc_2026_cal.yaml (1846.8 px) on 2026-10-27 (720bdd9). Re-derive the robot pack's intrinsics whenever a calibration changes.

Try it: run, check, record

The launcher normally starts the cameras. By hand, on the Orin:

# both ZED nodes and picture_taker
ros2 launch riptide_hardware2 zed.launch.py robot:=talos
# the URDFs (camera TF) and the EKF
ros2 launch riptide_hardware2 navigation.launch.py robot:=talos

From a laptop on the robot's network (joining from your own laptop):

# about 15 Hz (pretest: 8 or more)
ros2 topic hz /talos/ffc/zed_node/rgb/image_rect_color/compressed
# switch the detector to the down camera (false = forward)
ros2 service call /talos/set_camera_is_dfc std_srvs/srv/SetBool "{data: true}"
# which camera the detector is using now
ros2 topic echo --once /talos/detected_objects --field header.frame_id
# stop picture_taker pulling raw stereo (true to resume)
ros2 service call /talos/picture_taker/enable_subscription std_srvs/srv/SetBool "{data: false}"
  • Record video: the viewer's Recording panel calls each ZED node's start_svo_rec / stop_svo_rec (commands below) and writes one .svo2 per camera on the robot. It keeps both eyes; with compression_mode 0 this wrapper version records H.265. These files also become training data.
  • Replay by setting svo.svo_path (commented out in ffc_config.yaml): the ZED node plays the file instead of the camera.
  • Lighter: a bag of the /compressed topics, JPEGs only, no depth.
# as the Recording panel does it
ros2 service call /talos/ffc/zed_node/start_svo_rec zed_msgs/srv/StartSvoRec \
  "{svo_filename: /home/ros/svos/test_ffc.svo2}"
ros2 service call /talos/ffc/zed_node/stop_svo_rec std_srvs/srv/Trigger
