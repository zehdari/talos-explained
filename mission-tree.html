Autonomy · Chapter 3

The competition mission

One behaviour tree runs Talos's whole RoboSub run. This chapter reads it task by task, and lets you open every node of the real file while a Nereus run replays on top of it.

The short version
  • One file, RepairCompMpcTree.xml: kill switch, dive, gate, slalom, the pinger, torpedoes and table (the pinger picks which first), bins, magnets, then home through the gate.
  • Every task is wrapped in ForceSuccess. A task that fails or times out never stops the run; Talos moves on to the next one.
  • Each task has the same shape: tell mapping what to look for, search until its estimate is tight, lock the map, then move and act on that frozen estimate.
  • Between tasks Talos flies one path through waypoints that are fixed in mapping's config.
  • In Nereus the run takes 8½ minutes from the kill switch, more than 3 of them at the table.

Chapter 1 explained behaviour trees and Chapter 2 the nodes Talos has. This is the tree that uses them: trees/RepairCompMpcTree.xml in riptide_autonomy (branch autonomy-mapping). The perception side of the same tasks (frames, targets, covariance checks) is in Frames, targets and the mission tree.

The mission in one picture

How the file is built

  • A library plus a mission. The file is about 4,100 lines with 87 subtree definitions: Groot saves the team's whole subtree library into every tree file. The mission reaches 42 of them.
  • Repair or rescue. The main tree logs MISSION: REPAIR and runs CompMissionTreeMpc with repair_mission = 1. RescueCompMpcTree.xml is the same file with 0, which picks the other gate side, the blood torpedo holes and bin vinyls, and other images at the table.
  • Every subtree has its own blackboard (__shared_blackboard="false"). Each attribute on a SubTree maps one of its ports to a key of the caller, never a value. That is why the file is full of lines like <SetBlackboard output_key="1" value="1"/>: to pass the number 1, the caller first stores it under the key "1".
  • Who runs it. doTask ticks the tree every 30 ms until it returns SUCCESS or FAILURE, then disables the controller and sets the LEDs to pulsing green or red (DoTask.cpp). You start it from the autonomy panel of the Nereus viewer, in the sim and on the robot alike.
  • One step can end the mission early: the dive. Everything after it is inside a ForceSuccess or can't fail.
  • The "error" number. Every convergence check compares mapping's error for an object with a threshold: the root of the summed squared variances, the angular ones divided by 2π (MapConverged, getCovariance). It is in m², not metres. With the same spread σ in x, y and z (and the angle terms left out) it is √3·σ², so σ = √(error/√3): 0.0005 is about 1.7 cm, 0.0025 about 4 cm, 0.02 about 11 cm, 0.3 about 42 cm. The σ values on this page are worked out that way (Tree nodes that talk to mapping).
  • One dead block. Right after the pinger, a ForceSuccess sets target_image to buoy or compass. No subtree is passed that key, so nothing reads it; the table picks its own image.

Before the gate

Nothing moves until the kill switch is in, and nothing the cameras see moves the map until a task asks for it.

Kill switch
WaitForKillSwitch: LEDs solid red, wait 5 s, then read state/kill until it says not killed (with a 1 s wait after the read that says so). Then blink blue and wait 5 s more (XML). It runs once, at the start.
Prep
PrepForRun: lock the map with no target, reload the actuators (command/actuator/notify_reload), disarm.
Dive
Hold the heading and ask for −0.56 m, which PrimitiveMovePosition pushes down to its −0.75 m minimum depth. It counts as done within 25 cm, with 60 s to get there; then level (15 s).
If it fails
The dive is the one step with no ForceSuccess: if Talos isn't at depth after 60 s, the whole mission ends with FAILURE.
In Nereus
Kill switch in at 0:33, dive done at 0:41, at about −0.52 m. That counts: −0.52 m is within 25 cm of −0.75 m. Nereus spawns Talos at about −0.33 m, so the dive takes 2 s.

Gate

GateTreeMpc flies the gate from its prior. The map is still locked, so a detection can't move the gate; its search block starts with an AlwaysFailure named "Comment out following", so neither mini-search runs.

Side
Repair goes through gate_repair. Where gate_rescue sits in that frame decides the slalom side: positive y sets sla_pass_on_left = 1.
Moves
One FollowPath: 1 m in front of gate_repair_frame, then through it, 1.1 m below the frame (−1.35 m in Nereus), 60 s. After the flip, 1 m out the back (30 s).
Flip
FlipTree is the pre-MPC flip. It first disables the controller, then fails to set controller__stunt__safe_mode (the MPC has no such parameter) and gives up after 10 s. FlipTreeMpc, the MPC version (a path, with the DVL spoofed as still), is in the file but never called, and untested in the mission.
In Nereus
0:41 to 1:04. During the 10 s with the thrusters off, Talos floated up from −1.35 m to −1.03 m.

Slalom

SlalomPathMpc looks once, locks the map, and flies the three pole sets as one path.

Detector
set_slalom_type = slalom_parent: the name the detector publishes slalom poles under.
Target
slalom_parent, by MiniSearchMpc: forward camera 2.5 m in front, wait 5 s, then check the error every second until it is under 0.3 (σ ≈ 42 cm) or 15 s pass. Then lock the map.
Moves
One FollowPath (180 s). For the front, middle and back sets: 0.75 m before, level with it, 0.375 m after, all 0.8 m to the side (a quarter of the 3.2 m row) on the side the gate chose. Then on to pre_torpedo_pose_frame, holding the slalom's heading.
Why one path
The MPC flies through waypoints without stopping and settles only on the last. Each waypoint stays in its pole set's frame, so the path moves if mapping moves the frames.
In Nereus
1:04 to 1:39. The first check already read 0.00018.

The pinger picks the order

RandomPingerTree listens at two spots and decides whether the torpedo or the table comes first.

Listen
Lock the map. Move to pre_torpedo_pose_frame, sample the hydrophone 10 s (/talos/acoustics/start_sample, stop_sample), move to pre_table_pose_frame, sample 10 s. These moves use the older point-to-point Move subtree, not FollowPath.
Decide
riptide_acoustics compares the two windows' pinger amplitude and publishes buffer_0_closer: true when the first stop (the torpedo's) was louder. The tree reads it as pinger_is_table. True: table, then torpedo. False: torpedo, then table.
If it fails
If a service call fails, the read never happens, pinger_is_table stays empty, and CompareNums reads an empty port as 0: torpedo first. If the services work but no answer is published within 3 s, GetBoolTopic writes false and fails: torpedo first too.
In Nereus
1:39 to 2:12. The sim has no pinger, so the second stop_sample fails ("Cannot compare, buffer 0 has 0 samples"), its two retries get "Not sampling", GetBoolTopic never runs, and the run goes torpedo first.

Between tasks

Talos gets from one task to the next on a single FollowPath through fixed waypoints. Nothing is searched on the way (CompMissionTreeMpc).

The waypoints
pre_torpedo_pose, pre_table_pose, pre_bin_pose, pre_return_home: spots near each task and on the way home, set before the run in init_data of mapping's config. Mapping publishes each as a frame (pre_torpedo_pose_frame and so on). post_table_pose and post_bin_pose hang 0.5 m above their pre_ frame.
Do they move?
No detector sees them, but like every prop they are placed relative to where mapping thinks the course sits, so they shift with it (map_estimator.py; Frames, targets and the mission tree, RoboSub and the course).
Depth
Every transit is kept between 0.75 m and 2.5 m deep (min_depth, max_depth).
Torpedo first
  1. Target torpedo (map unlocked), then to pre_torpedo facing torpedo_frame, 60 s.
  2. After the torpedo: pre_torpedo → pre_table → post_table, holding pre_torpedo's heading, 120 s.
  3. After the table: post_table → pre_table → pre_bin → post_bin, holding the current heading, 120 s.
Table first
  1. pre_table → post_table, holding the current heading, 120 s.
  2. After the table: target torpedo (map unlocked), then post_table → pre_table → pre_torpedo, facing torpedo_frame on the last leg, 120 s.
  3. After the torpedo: pre_torpedo → pre_bin → post_bin, holding pre_torpedo's heading, 120 s.
If it fails
Each transit has its own ForceSuccess: if the path times out, the next task starts from wherever Talos stopped.
In Nereus
To the torpedo 2:12 to 2:24, to the table 2:45 to 3:02, to the bins 6:18 to 6:35.

Torpedoes

Map the board on the way, search until it is tight, then map both holes from a stand-off and shoot them one after the other.

On the way
Target torpedo (map unlocked), then a FollowPath to pre_torpedo_pose_frame that keeps facing torpedo_frame (60 s).
Search
Forward camera. SearchMpc until the board's error is under 0.0025 (σ ≈ 4 cm) or 100 s (below). Even if it times out, Talos shoots.
Holes
Arm. Stand off 1.5 m in front of the board, target fire_hole_large and fire_hole_small together, hold up to 30 s until both are under 0.0005 (σ ≈ 1.7 cm), lock the map.
Shot 1
Put torpedo_0_link 1.1 m, then 0.6 m from the large hole as one path, wait for the controller to settle, fire. Path and wait get two tries, each up to 60 s of path and 60 s of settling, so a shot can come up to about 4 minutes late, but it always comes.
Shot 2
Slide torpedo_1_link to 0.6 m from the small hole, wait (two tries again), fire. Disarm.
Settled means
WaitForController: the MPC's controller/scale/trust above 0.985 on every axis, and the launcher's y offset in the hole's frame under 1 cm, within 60 s. Trust rises toward 1 while Talos sits within 2 cm and 2° of its setpoint and nearly still (settle_trust.hpp).
In Nereus
2:24 to 2:45. The board was centred 2.8 s after the search began; both holes converged 1.5 s after being targeted.

Table

NewTableTreeMpc maps the two baskets, fits the table to them, then sorts four items into the baskets with the claw.

Setup
Down camera. Table pair mode off. All six table props (helmet, warning, bandage, pill, nut_and_bolt, plug) become background targets: mapped whenever seen.
Baskets
DownwardSearchMpc for helmet, then warning: to 0.0005 each, 60 s, camera 1.5 m above. If both pass in one 1 s check, center_table_on_baskets moves the table onto them; otherwise it keeps its prior.
Four sorts
bandage and pill into helmet, nut_and_bolt and plug into warning. Each is a pickup, a surface and a drop.
Pickup
Search the item to 0.5 (just "seen"), 180 s. Hover the camera 1.5 m above it for up to 5 s until 0.0005. Lock the map, open the claw, bring claw_link 2 m above, then 15 cm, then onto the item (22 mm higher for the shorter bandage and plug), waiting for the controller (trust 0.95, 1 cm) at the last two. Close, and 2 s later back up.
Surface
SurfaceTree: over the table's centre, command z −0.3 m until above −0.35 m (15 s), then back down.
Drop
Claw over the basket at its current height, then 2 m above it, then 15 cm. Wait for the controller, lock, open for 0.3 s, back up to 2 m, close.
Finish
Go over the midpoint of the baskets (the tree asks for 1.5 m above it, but Move keeps to the −0.75 m minimum depth, as for the dive; in Nereus the request was z −0.02 m and Talos held −0.75 m), face the hammer_and_wrench image (rescue: sos), spin one full turn per item sorted, face it again and surface. Disarm, clear the background targets, forward camera.
Claw moves
The claw and surface moves use the older Move subtree (point-to-point setpoints), not FollowPath. The claw itself is command/actuator/claw_move_s: seconds to run it, + opens, − closes.
In Nereus
3:02 to 6:18, all four items. The table moved 5 cm and turned 0.6° to fit the baskets. Each sort took about 34 s.

Bins

The bin has two identical fire vinyls. The binary classifier splits them into bin_target1 and bin_target2; the tree drops one marker on each.

Start
Down camera. start_binary_classifier on fire (rescue: blood), 3 tries. If it won't start, the bins are skipped.
Target 1
DownwardSearchMpc to 0.02 (σ ≈ 11 cm), 120 s, camera 2.15 m above. Arm.
Drop 1
ShootDownwardObjectMpc: hover the camera over it up to 3 s until 0.0005, freeze the classifier's buffer, lock, put droppers_link 1 m above, wait (trust 0.90, 1 cm, 30 s), fire the dropper (it checks one is left first).
Target 2
start_binary_classifier_second, then the same search and drop for bin_target2. Stop the classifier, disarm.
Freeze refused
The buffer freeze before the second drop is refused: by then the classifier is on the second vinyl, and mapping only freezes while tracking the first ("Can only lock the buffer while tracking the first instance"; binary classifier). The call is wrapped, so the drop goes ahead on the unfrozen estimate.
One difference
Here the search and the drop share one sequence: if bin_target2 isn't under 0.02 after 120 s, the second marker is never dropped. The first drop happens whatever its search found.
In Nereus
6:35 to 7:07. The second target needed the spiral: 8 s to come into view.

How Talos searches

Three search subtrees do most of the looking. All of them end when mapping's error for the object drops below the threshold, or when time runs out.

MiniSearchMpc
Slalom, magnets. Park the forward camera in front of the object, set the target, wait 5 s, then check every second. Once the error is under 0.1 it re-centres in front of the new estimate.
SearchMpc
Torpedo board. Sweep arcs round the estimate while panning the heading. As soon as the object is seen, chase it square on (CenterDetection) and hold it in view up to 8 s. A sweep that saw nothing makes the next one wider.
DownwardSearchMpc
Baskets, items, bins. Skip if already converged. Fly the down camera over the estimate at full speed; once the object is in view, re-aim at its latest detection every 0.5 s (DownwardTrack). Otherwise spiral out at 0.2 m/s.

When something fails

What goes wrongWhat the tree does
A whole task failsIts ForceSuccess turns it into a success; the next task starts.
A search times outSearches are wrapped too: Talos shoots, grabs or drops on the estimate it has. One exception: the second bin target, whose drop is skipped.
The controller won't settleEvery WaitForController before a shot, grab or touch is wrapped: it acts anyway.
A FollowPath times out, or makes no progress for 10 sThe rest of that sequence is skipped. A gate approach that times out skips the flip and the exit.
An actuator call failsArming, torpedoes and droppers are all inside a ForceSuccess named "REMOVE ME": they can't fail the tree, so nothing retries them.
The table's last move failsIts last two RelativeMoveNoYaw moves aren't wrapped. If one fails, the rest of the table tree is skipped: Talos stays armed, the background targets are never cleared, and the forward camera isn't switched back before the bins.
A camera switch failsThe sequence it starts is skipped: the torpedo search, the bins, or that one table item.
A classifier won't startThe bins (or the magnets) are skipped.
The dive doesn't reach depthThe mission ends with FAILURE after 60 s.
The kill switch is pulledThe thrusters stop, but the tree keeps running (below).
The kill switch does not stop the mission
  • The tree keeps running. doTask's kill check is commented out (DoTask.cpp), and WaitForKillSwitch only runs at the start.
  • It hurries on while killed. The MPC cuts thrust and aborts the path it was flying ("vehicle killed"), and aborts every new path the same way (mpc_controller_node.cpp). Settle waits run out their time with trust at 0. The ForceSuccess wrappers move the mission on from task to task.
  • Putting the switch back in resumes the run at whatever step the tree has reached, possibly several tasks later, and Talos flies whatever the tree commands next.
  • To end a run, stop the tree: Stop in the viewer's Autonomy panel, or KILL in its command bar (Running a tree). That needs the tether; untethered, keep the switch out until you can stop the tree.

What the LEDs say

In the pool the LEDs are often the only sign of what the tree is doing. The tree sets them with SetStatus (SetStatus.btaction.hpp); doTask sets the last one when the tree ends (DoTask.cpp).

LEDsMeaning
Solid redwaiting: waiting for the kill switch.
Slow blue flashstarting: switch in, 5 s to go.
Solid orangesearching: a search is running.
Slow magenta flashaligning: moving into place for a task's action.
Fast blue flashperforming: doing it: the gate pass, a shot, a drop, the claw, a magnet touch.
Breathing greenThe tree ended with SUCCESS.
Breathing redThe tree ended with FAILURE, or was stopped.
Fast red flashAn error while ticking the tree aborted it (DoTask.cpp).

The mission never sets moving (solid green); only the old BuoyTree subtree does. Between tasks the LEDs keep whatever the last task left.

Things to watch

  • The gate's flip turns the controller off. For about 10 s in the middle of the gate, Talos has no control (above). FlipTreeMpc is the MPC version, but untested in the mission. Switching to it, or dropping the flip, removes the 10 s with no control.
  • The pinger's meaning looks backwards. pinger_is_table is true when the pinger was louder at the torpedo stop, and true means table first. The rule rewards scoring first at the task the active pinger marks (RoboSub and the course), so a louder torpedo stop should mean torpedo first. Settle it before a competition.
  • The pinger's default mode. The launch file runs riptide_acoustics in max mode, and compare_max_buffers only sets its answer through the fallback to averages. When the two maxima differ by 1000 or more it leaves buffer0Closer at its old value (false at start: torpedo first).
  • The second torpedo doesn't wait. FireTorpedo calls WaitForActuators with timeout_secs="10", but never sets the key "10" (FireDropper does). The wait gives up at once ("Actuators busy!") and the second shot follows about 6 s after the first.
  • Nereus refused both second shots in this run. The second torpedo (6 s after the first) and the second dropper (19 s after the first) both got "Release group is busy", and the tree went on as if they had fired. That message is Nereus's cooldown check: torpedoes and droppers share one release group (mechanisms.cpp), and its cooldown is only 0.5 s (robot.yaml). So neither gap should have hit it, and the log doesn't show why it did. The missing wait isn't the cause: in the other Nereus run, the one the Actuators chapter uses, the two drops also came 19 s apart and both landed. That run's second torpedo was refused for a different reason: a task reset, which disarms, just after the first shot (How the trees use them).
  • Drops skip their approach. dropObjectMpc wraps its DownwardMove in a RetryUntilSuccessfulOrTimeout with num_seconds="0", which fails before running it. The claw goes straight to the basket's current estimate.
  • Empty ports read as zero. A port mapped to a key nobody set reads as empty, and most nodes then use a default. The run's log warns about four: the pinger's CompareNums, the FireTorpedo wait, the magnet task's camera switch (is_dfc="0", no key "0"; it still ends on the forward camera) and its MiniSearchMpc (prox_y="0"). Read "does not have a value for required port" warnings after every run.
  • Only y is checked. WaitForController checks the link's sideways (y) offset in the target frame, not its height or range.
  • Perception side. The one-second basket check, pickups that may not refine, and a bins classifier that can block the magnets are in Frames, targets and the mission tree.

The other trees

Everything in trees/, one line each. "MPC era" trees move with FollowPath; the older ones move with Move/SmartMovePosition setpoints, were written for the controllers before riptide_mpc, and many target an older course. To change or test a tree, see Writing and testing trees.
