# Glossary source: perception, control, autonomy and running Talos. Format: see start.txt.

## Detector
aka: yolo_orientation, yolo_orientation.py, tensorrt.launch.py, tensor_detector
part: perception
slug: yolo-orientation
The node that runs the YOLO model on one camera's images and turns each mask into a 3D pose with the depth image (`yolo_orientation`). Its output is `/talos/detected_objects`.
> perception/detector.html#model
> perception/detector.html#outputs

## YOLO
exp: You Only Look Once
aka: YOLO26n-seg, Ultralytics, segmentation model
part: perception
The family of fast neural-network detectors Talos uses, from Ultralytics. Each camera has its own YOLO26n-seg model, which returns a class, confidence, box and pixel mask per object.
> perception/detector.html#model
> perception/training.html#what

## Segmentation
aka: mask, instance segmentation
part: perception
Finding exactly which pixels belong to each object (its mask), not just a box round it. Talos needs the mask to fit a surface and read which way a prop faces.
> perception/training.html#what
> perception/pose.html#sample

## Class
aka: class name
part: perception
What the detector says an object is, such as `torpedo`, `fire` or `nut_and_bolt`. The names match the course's props and are the names mapping and the trees use.
> perception/detector.html#model
> robosub.html#course

## TensorRT
aka: .engine, engine
part: perception
NVIDIA's compiler for neural networks. On the Orin the detector runs the model as a TensorRT `.engine`, much faster than PyTorch; an engine only works on the GPU and version that built it.
> perception/detector.html#tensorrt
> perception/training.html#deploy

## SAM
exp: Segment Anything Model
part: perception
A labelling model the team uses to draw masks on real prop videos, which become YOLO training data.
> perception/training.html#label

## Augmentation
aka: augmented
part: perception
Making more training images from the same ones: rotating, zooming, mirroring and pasting props onto unrelated photos.
> perception/training.html#augment

## Synthetic data
aka: nereus-dataset, dataset
part: perception
Training images rendered by Nereus, which labels every pixel for free. The 2026 forward set has 10 250 images, including empty backgrounds.
> perception/training.html#synthetic
> nereus.html#datasets

## Intrinsics
aka: focal length, camera matrix, K
part: perception
A camera's focal length and image centre, in pixels: what turns a direction in front of the lens into a pixel.
> perception/calibration.html#pinhole

## Lens distortion
aka: distortion
part: perception
How a real lens bends straight lines, measured in calibration and undone before the image is used.
> perception/calibration.html#distortion

## Calibration (camera)
aka: camera calibration, underwater calibration
part: perception
slug: camera-calibration
Measuring a camera's intrinsics, distortion and stereo geometry. Talos's cameras are calibrated underwater, because water and the flat window change all three.
> perception/calibration.html#underwater
> perception/calibration.html#procedure

## ChArUco board
aka: ChArUco
part: perception
A chessboard with printed ArUco markers in its white squares. Each corner is identified, so a calibration works even when the board is only partly in view.
> perception/calibration.html#charuco

## Extrinsics
part: perception
Where a camera sits relative to something else: its two lenses relative to each other, or the camera on the robot.
> perception/calibration.html#stereo
> perception/calibration.html#extrinsics

## Rectification
aka: rectified, image_rect_color
part: perception
Warping a stereo pair so a point sits on the same row in both images. The ZED SDK does it on the GPU; the stack uses the rectified images.
> perception/calibration.html#stereo
> cameras.html#pipeline

## Stereo depth
aka: disparity, baseline
part: perception
Depth from two lenses: a near point shifts more between the left and right images than a far one. Depth is focal length × [[baseline]] ÷ that shift, so error grows with range squared.
> cameras.html#stereo
> perception/calibration.html#stereo
> perception/noise.html#stereo

## Baseline
The distance between a stereo camera's two lenses: about 50 mm on the ZED X Mini.
> cameras.html#two-cameras

## Depth image
aka: depth map, depth_registered
part: perception
An image whose pixels are distances in metres instead of colours, aligned with the colour image. Pixels where stereo failed are NaN or zero.
> cameras.html#topics
> perception/pose.html#backproject

## Point cloud
A set of 3D points, here from a depth image. The viewer can show the cameras' clouds; they are heavy over the tether.
> nereus.html#cameras
> viewer.html#seeing

## Back-projection
part: perception
Running the camera model backwards: a pixel plus its depth gives a 3D point in the camera frame.
> perception/pose.html#backproject

## Depth gate
part: perception
Dropping mask samples whose depth is far from the median, so background seen through or round a prop doesn't bend its pose.
> perception/pose.html#gate

## Plane fit
aka: normal, SVD
part: perception
Fitting a flat surface to a prop's 3D points. Its normal, the direction straight out of the surface, says which way the prop faces.
> perception/pose.html#plane

## Detection
aka: detected_objects, Detection3DArray
part: perception
One object seen in one frame: class, confidence and a pose, published per frame on `/talos/detected_objects`. Its covariance is left at zero for mapping to decide.
> perception/detector.html#outputs
> perception/noise.html#model

## Mahalanobis distance
aka: chi-squared, gate
part: perception
Distance measured in standard deviations along the direction of the miss. Mapping rejects a detection whose distance from its track is too big to be chance.
> perception/tracking.html#gating
> perception/math.html#reading

## Track
aka: tracker
part: perception
Mapping's running estimate of one prop, a Kalman filter fed by its detections. A track is targeted, established and finally confirmed before trees trust it.
> perception/tracking.html#update
> perception/tracking.html#confirm

## Confirmed
aka: targeted, established
part: perception
A track that has had enough agreeing detections while targeted, and hasn't been asked to re-check since. Setting a target clears it.
> perception/tracking.html#confirm

## Mapping
aka: riptide_mapping2, mapping.py, map_estimator
part: perception
slug: riptide-mapping2
The node that fuses detections into one estimate per prop, starting from the prior map, and publishes each prop as a TF frame (`riptide_mapping2`).
> perception/map.html#why
> perception/frames.html#published

## Prior map
aka: prior, init_data, config.yaml
part: perception
Where each prop is expected to be before Talos sees it, in mapping's `config.yaml`. Measured on each course and edited in the viewer's prior map editor.
> perception/map.html#model
> viewer.html#mapping

## Offset
aka: map → offset
part: perception
Mapping's one shared shift of the whole course against odometry, published as the TF `map → offset`. Every map prop hangs below it, so seeing one prop moves the unseen ones too.
> perception/map.html#model
> perception/frames.html#tree

## Deviation
part: perception
How far one prop sits from where the layout says, on top of the shared offset. A prop that disagrees a lot is blamed alone instead of dragging the course.
> perception/map.html#model
> perception/map.html#robust

## Fix
part: perception
A finished track of one prop, stamped with the odometry distance and time it was seen at. Two fixes of a prop far apart measure odometry drift.
> perception/map.html#fixes

## Mapping target
aka: mapping_target, target, background target
part: perception
Which props mapping fuses right now, set by the tree. Only targeted detections can confirm a prop; other props in view still improve as background targets.
> perception/frames.html#targets
> viewer.html#mapping

## Lock map
aka: lock the map
part: perception
Telling mapping to ignore all detections and freeze every estimate, so Talos acts on a fixed position while it shoots, drops or grabs.
> perception/frames.html#targets
> mission-tree.html#overview

## Binary classifier
part: perception
Mapping's helper that splits two identical-looking props (the bins' two symbols, the two magnets) into target 1 and target 2.
> perception/frames.html#binary
> mission-tree.html#bins

## Composite
aka: torpedo board, pairs
part: perception
A prop made of several detected pieces, such as the torpedo board and its holes or a slalom row of poles, handled together.
> perception/composites.html#pairs
> perception/composites.html#torpedo

## camera_faker
The simulated ZED cameras of the previous simulator, riptide_simulator, which Nereus replaced. Kept off the robot with [[DEPLOY_IGNORE]].
> code-map.html#explorer

## MPC
exp: Model predictive control
aka: NMPC, nonlinear MPC, riptide_mpc
part: control
A controller that, every tick, predicts Talos's motion with a model for a short horizon, chooses the thrust plan that best follows the reference, and sends only the first step. Talos's is [[riptide_mpc]].
> control/index.html#loop
> control/mpc.html#problem

## riptide_mpc
aka: mpc_controller
part: control
The controller Talos flies with: a nonlinear MPC on a Fossen model, 20 times a second, planning 1.5 s ahead and writing thruster RPMs straight to CAN.
> control/index.html#files
> commanding.html#flow

## Control tick
aka: control period, 50 ms
part: control
One cycle of the MPC: every 50 ms it reads the state, solves, and sends eight thrusts. In Nereus it is on sim time, so it has to keep up with the simulator.
> control/index.html#loop
> nereus.html#time

## Horizon
aka: stage, prediction horizon
part: control
How far ahead the MPC plans: 30 stages of 50 ms, 1.5 s. The viewer's MPC path overlay draws it.
> control/mpc.html#state
> viewer.html#seeing

## Receding horizon
part: control
Plan the whole horizon, use only the first 50 ms, then plan again from the new state. The rest of the plan only seeds the next solve.
> control/mpc.html#receding

## Reference
aka: setpoint
part: control
Where the controller is told to be at each moment. A new setpoint isn't chased directly: a smooth reference glides to it.
> control/reference.html#why
> commanding.html#goals

## S-curve
aka: motion profile, jerk-limited profile
part: control
The jerk-limited path the reference takes to a new setpoint, so speed and acceleration build up and die down smoothly.
> control/reference.html#scurve

## Path
aka: path_plan, PathSegment
part: control
A list of waypoints (lines, arcs, rounded corners) flown as one curve with one speed profile, through [[follow_path]].
> control/reference.html#paths
> commanding.html#paths

## follow_path
aka: FollowPath action
part: control
The MPC's action: fly through a list of poses without stopping and settle on the last. Trees send it with the [[FollowPath]] node.
> commanding.html#paths
> bt-nodes.html#moving

## Governor
aka: reference governor
part: control
Slows the reference when Talos falls behind it, so the controller isn't asked to catch up with something running away.
> control/reference.html#governor

## Feedforward
aka: u_ff
part: control
The thrust the model says the reference needs (to hold depth against buoyancy, to push through drag). The MPC penalizes deviation from it, not thrust itself, so it doesn't lag.
> control/mpc.html#feedforward

## Cost
aka: weights, Q
part: control
What the MPC minimizes: how far the plan is from the reference plus how much thrust deviates from the feedforward, each weighted. The weights depend on the mode.
> control/mpc.html#errors
> control/mpc.html#modes

## QP
exp: Quadratic program
part: control
An optimization with a quadratic cost and linear constraints. Each tick the MPC's problem is linearized into one QP in 240 numbers (30 stages × 8 thrusters).
> control/solver.html#condensing
> control/solver.html#qp

## Condensing
part: control
Eliminating the predicted states from the problem, since they follow from the commands, to leave a small dense QP in the thrusts alone.
> control/solver.html#condensing

## Projected Newton
part: control
The method that solves the MPC's QP with its ±24 N limits per thruster, usually in one or two iterations.
> control/solver.html#qp

## RTI
exp: Real-time iteration
aka: SQP, warm start
part: control
Taking one optimization step per tick instead of solving to the end, starting from last tick's plan shifted by one stage. It works because consecutive problems are nearly the same.
> control/solver.html#rti

## Jacobian
aka: finite differences, linearization
part: control
How each predicted state changes with a small change of state or command. riptide_mpc gets them by finite differences on the model.
> control/solver.html#jacobians

## Quaternion
part: control
Four numbers for an attitude, with no gimbal lock. The MPC perturbs it on the manifold (small rotations), since adding to a quaternion breaks it.
> control/solver.html#manifold
> commanding.html#terminal

## Fossen model
aka: equation of motion, marine dynamics
part: control
The standard equation of motion for a vehicle in water: mass and added mass, Coriolis, damping, and gravity with buoyancy. The MPC and Nereus share the same code for it.
> control/model.html#eom
> nereus.html#physics

## DOF
exp: Degrees of freedom
aka: 6 DOF, surge, sway, heave, roll, pitch, yaw
part: control
The six ways a body can move: surge, sway and heave (forward, sideways, up) and roll, pitch and yaw (turning about x, y, z).
> control/model.html#frames
> thrusters-hw.html#layout

## Wrench
aka: tau
part: control
A force and a torque together, six numbers. The thrusters' eight forces add up to one wrench on Talos.
> control/thrusters.html#matrix

## Added mass
part: control
Accelerating Talos also accelerates the water round it, so it behaves as if it had more mass than it does. Identified in the pool.
> control/model.html#mass
> control/identify.html#inertia

## Damping
aka: drag
part: control
The water's resistance, growing with speed (linear and quadratic terms). Identified in the pool.
> control/model.html#damping
> control/identify.html#drag

## Restoring forces
aka: righting moment
part: control
Gravity at the COM and buoyancy at the COB. With the COB above the COM they level Talos when it tilts.
> control/model.html#restoring

## Thruster matrix
aka: T, thrust allocation matrix
part: control
The 6 × 8 matrix that turns eight thruster forces into one wrench, built from the thrusters' positions and directions in CAD.
> control/thrusters.html#matrix

## Allocation
part: control
Turning a desired wrench into eight thruster forces. The MPC doesn't allocate to control: its decision variables are the forces themselves. It uses allocation only to compute its [[Feedforward|feedforward]] thrust, and `FEEDFORWARD` mode allocates the fixed wrench directly.
> control/thrusters.html#allocation

## Null space
part: control
Thruster patterns that push against each other and produce no net wrench. Identification uses them to test thrusters without moving Talos.
> control/thrusters.html#nullspace
> control/identify.html#session

## Thruster dynamics
aka: delay, lag, slew
part: control
A thruster's force arrives late: a 30 ms delay, then a lag of 60 to 80 ms, with a slew limit. The MPC plans through it with a replica of the thrusters.
> control/thrusters.html#dynamics
> control/mpc.html#delay

## Power budget
aka: 73 N
part: control
A cap on the sum of all eight thrusts (73 N). A plan over it is scaled down, keeping its direction.
> control/index.html#loop
> electrical.html#budget

## RK4
exp: Fourth-order Runge–Kutta
part: control
The integration step the model uses to predict the next state from the current one.
> control/model.html#integration

## Disturbance estimate
aka: offset-free, disturbance wrench
part: control
A wrench the MPC learns for whatever its model gets wrong (a little extra buoyancy, say) and adds to its predictions, so Talos holds position without a steady error.
> control/estimation.html#offsetfree

## MPC state estimator
aka: state_estimator, state_estimator.cpp, state_source
part: control
The MPC builds its own state straight from the IMU, gyro, DVL and depth sensor, because the [[EKF]]'s output arrives late for a controller planning 1.5 s ahead. The EKF only anchors x, y and heading, and re-seeds it after a large disagreement (over 1 m or 0.5 rad).
> control/estimation.html#why
> localization.html#users

## Trust
aka: settle trust, controller/scale/trust
part: control
A number from 0 to 1 per axis that says Talos has arrived and stayed. Trees wait for it (typically above 0.8) before the next step.
> control/estimation.html#trust
> commanding.html#goals

## System identification
aka: ident, pool_identify, identification
part: control
Fitting the numbers you can't measure on land (buoyancy, COB, drag, added mass, thruster gains) from a scripted pool session flown by the MPC.
> control/identify.html#premise
> pool-test.html#ident

## Statics
part: control
What it takes to hold still: net buoyancy and where the COB sits, the first thing identification fits.
> control/identify.html#statics

## ControllerCommand
aka: controller/linear, controller/angular
part: control
The message that sets the MPC's goal, one for the linear half and one for the angular half, each with a mode and a setpoint.
> commanding.html#modes
> interfaces.html

## Controller modes
aka: POSITION, VELOCITY, FEEDFORWARD, DISABLED, FF_body_force, controller/FF_body_force
part: control
`DISABLED` (no thrust), `FEEDFORWARD` (both halves: a fixed body wrench from `controller/FF_body_force` split straight onto the thrusters, no MPC), `VELOCITY` and `POSITION`. Either half disabled stops the whole controller.
> commanding.html#modes
> control/mpc.html#modes

## controller_overseer
aka: motion_enabled, controller/motion_enabled, setTeleop
part: control
A small Python node started with the controller. It watches the ESC telemetry and sets `controller/motion_enabled` false (zero thrust) after repeated faults or 2 s of silence. It also publishes the 1 Hz feedforward wrench and answers the viewer's `setTeleop` call.
> commanding.html#stops
> commanding.html#senders
> commanding.html#goals

## Teleop
part: control
Driving Talos by hand. Today the viewer's Motion panel is the teleop: typed targets, a drag gizmo or one-step-per-key moves.
> commanding.html#teleop
> viewer.html#motion

## Legacy controller
aka: hybrid, complete_controller, riptide_controllers2, Simulink
part: control
The old Simulink-built controller, no longer built. Launch files still default to `active_control_model:=hybrid`, which would start it, so nothing flies: pass `mpc`.
> commanding.html#legacy

## active_control_model
aka: mpc_model
part: control
The launch argument that picks the controller: `mpc` starts riptide_mpc. In Nereus, `mpc_model:=sim` gives the MPC the simulator's exact model.
> commanding.html#legacy
> nereus.html#mpc-model

## Behaviour tree
aka: BT, tree, behavior tree, mission tree
part: autonomy
The way Talos decides what to do: a tree of nodes in an XML file, ticked from the root. Control nodes pick which children run; leaves act or check.
> bt.html#ticks
> bt.html#why

## BehaviorTree.CPP
aka: BT.CPP, behaviortree_cpp_v3
part: autonomy
The C++ library Talos's trees run on, version 3. The team's own nodes are classes built on it.
> bt.html#ticks
> bt-nodes.html#kinds

## Tick
part: autonomy
One pass down a behaviour tree from the root. doTask ticks about every 30 ms until the root returns SUCCESS or FAILURE.
> bt.html#ticks
> bt-nodes.html#dotask

## Node status
aka: SUCCESS, FAILURE, RUNNING, IDLE
part: autonomy
What a tree node answers when ticked: `SUCCESS`, `FAILURE` or `RUNNING` (not done, tick me again). `IDLE` means not started.
> bt.html#ticks

## Sequence
aka: ReactiveSequence
part: autonomy
A control node that ticks its children in order and stops at the first that fails ("do all of these"). A ReactiveSequence re-checks the earlier children every tick.
> bt.html#control

## Fallback
part: autonomy
A control node that tries its children in order until one succeeds ("try until one works").
> bt.html#control

## Mapping nodes
aka: SetMappingTarget, MapConverged, SeenRecently, CenterDetection
part: autonomy
The tree nodes a search is built from. `SetMappingTarget` tells mapping which prop to fuse. `SeenRecently` (a detection within the last 0.5 s, in the searches) and `MapConverged` (mapping's error under a threshold) are conditions re-checked every tick, so the sweep beneath them stops the moment either passes. `CenterDetection` then brings the prop to the middle of the image.
> bt-nodes.html#perception
> perception/frames.html#trees

## IfThenElse
part: autonomy
A control node ("if, then, else"): it ticks the first child; if that succeeds, the second, otherwise the third. The competition tree ([[RepairCompMpcTree]]) uses it 55 times, mostly to pick numbers, such as the sweep size in a search. Not the same as a [[Fallback]].
> bt.html#control

## Decorator
aka: ForceSuccess, RetryUntilSuccessfulOrTimeout, Repeat
part: autonomy
A node with one child that changes how it runs or what it returns. `ForceSuccess` wraps every competition task, so a failed task doesn't end the run.
> bt.html#decorators

## Action node
aka: condition, async action, UWRTActionNode
part: autonomy
A leaf that does something (an action) or checks something (a condition). Slow ones are async: they start on one tick, return RUNNING and check their ROS reply on later ticks.
> bt-nodes.html#kinds
> bt.html#ros

## Halt
aka: onHalted
part: autonomy
Stopping a RUNNING node because its parent no longer needs it. Halting FollowPath cancels its MPC goal.
> bt.html#ticks
> bt.html#ros

## Blackboard
aka: SetBlackboard
part: autonomy
The key-value store that the nodes of one tree share. Nodes read and write it through ports.
> bt.html#blackboard
> bt-nodes.html#ports

## Port
part: autonomy
A named input or output of a node type. `seconds="5"` is a value; `seconds="{hold_secs}"` reads a blackboard key. Every port is a string, converted when read. For the robot's left side, see [[port-starboard|Port, starboard, bow, stern]].
> bt.html#blackboard
> bt-workflow.html#ports

## Subtree
aka: SubTree, remapping
part: autonomy
A tree called from another tree like a node. It gets its own blackboard; its ports map to the caller's key names, which is why trees write keys like `"15" = "15"`.
> bt.html#subtrees
> bt-workflow.html#new-subtree

## Groot
aka: TreeNodesModel, .fbl, btlogs
part: autonomy
The graphical editor for the tree XML (the team's fork), and the replay tool for the `~/btlogs/*.fbl` log each run writes.
> bt-workflow.html#editing
> bt.html#ticks

## Workspace file
aka: workspace.xml
part: autonomy
The Groot workspace that lists every node and its ports, so the editor's palette matches the C++.
> bt-workflow.html#workspace

## doTask
aka: DoTask.cpp, autonomy/run_tree, /talos/autonomy
part: autonomy
The tree runner node, `/talos/autonomy`: give its `run_tree` action a tree file and it ticks it until it ends. The viewer's Autonomy panel calls it.
> bt-nodes.html#dotask
> bt-workflow.html#run

## tree_stack
aka: autonomy/tree_stack
part: autonomy
A topic with the names of the running tree nodes, root first. The viewer shows it while a tree runs.
> bt-nodes.html#dotask

## checkBT
part: autonomy
The team's checker for tree files: nodes against the C++, port names and directions, unbalanced braces. Run it on your machine; it rewrites the files it checks.
> bt-workflow.html#checkbt

## btassistant.py
aka: BT assistant
part: autonomy
A helper that creates new node files from templates, rebuilds riptide_autonomy2 and runs the checks.
> bt-workflow.html#assistant

## UwrtBtNode
part: autonomy
The base class every custom tree node inherits next to BT.CPP's, which gives it the ROS node and a TF helper.
> bt-nodes.html#kinds

## FollowPath
aka: AddWaypoint, AddArc
part: autonomy
The tree node that sends a path to the MPC's follow_path action. `AddWaypoint` and `AddArc` build the path string piece by piece.
> bt-nodes.html#moving
> bt-nodes.html#waypoints

## Waypoint string
part: autonomy
FollowPath's whole path in one string: `frame: x,y,z,yaw | options; …`. A waypoint in a prop's frame moves with mapping's estimate of that prop.
> bt-nodes.html#waypoints

## WaitForController
aka: PublishToController, KillController
part: autonomy
Subtrees that wait for the MPC's trust and distance to say Talos has arrived. PublishToController sends a setpoint; KillController sends DISABLED.
> commanding.html#senders
> bt-nodes.html#moving

## Search subtrees
aka: SearchMpc, MiniSearchMpc, DownwardSearchMpc
part: autonomy
The subtrees that look for a prop until mapping's error is under a threshold or time runs out: sweeps with the forward camera, spirals with the downward one.
> mission-tree.html#search
> bt-nodes.html#perception

## ComputeFrameAlignment
part: autonomy
The node that works out where to put Talos so a tool frame (a torpedo tube, the claw) lines up with a target.
> bt-nodes.html#frames

## SetStatus
part: autonomy
The node that sets the status light's colour and pattern from a tree.
> bt-nodes.html#leds
> mission-tree.html#leds

## RepairCompMpcTree
aka: RepairCompMpcTree.xml, RescueCompMpcTree, CompMissionTreeMpc

The competition tree, about 4 100 lines: it sets `repair_mission = 1` and runs CompMissionTreeMpc through gate, slalom, pinger, torpedo, table, bins and home. RescueCompMpcTree is the same for the other role.

The blackboard key that picks the role: 1 for Survey & Repair, 0 for Search & Rescue. It is the only difference between the two competition trees.

The subtree the competition and prequal trees start with: it sets the status light and waits until `state/kill` reads false, that is, until the switch is in.

## HIL
exp: Hardware in the loop
part: ops
The Orin runs the real stack while a desk computer simulates the world, to test the real computer and its load. Not yet confirmed end to end with Nereus, and the MPC still drives the real ESCs, so the kill switch stays out.

## nereus-sim
aka: physics_simulator
part: ops
Nereus's simulator: C++ physics, sensors, cameras, mechanisms, props and scoring, with a ROS 2 bridge. It plays the hardware so the rest of the stack runs unchanged.
> nereus.html#what
> nereus.html#physics

## Pack
aka: packs, resolved.json
part: ops
A folder of YAML (plus meshes) that describes one thing in Nereus: the robot, a pool, the course, the ROS bridge. They are checked and merged into one `resolved.json` per run.
> nereus.html#packs

## Scenario
part: ops
The pack that picks one robot, pool and course and places everything, such as `talos_uwrt` or `talos_uwrt_rpac`.
> nereus.html#course
> perception/workflow.html#scenario

## Bridge
aka: stream, bridge.yaml
part: ops
Nereus's ROS node. Its pack lists each topic as a stream: direction, type and which simulator value fills which field.
> nereus.html#bridge

## Sim tick
aka: real-time factor
part: ops
Nereus advances in fixed 2 ms ticks and publishes `/clock` each one. The real-time factor sets how fast that runs against the wall clock.
> nereus.html#physics
> nereus.html#time

## Nereus viewer
aka: nereus-viewer, viewer
part: ops
The team's GUI for both the simulator and the real robot: a 3D pool view plus operator panels (Enable and KILL, Motion, Autonomy, Mapping, cameras). It replaced RViz.
> viewer.html#what
> viewer.html#tour

## Enable
aka: KILL button
part: ops
The viewer's buttons for kill slot 1. Enable latches, so a run survives the tether coming off; KILL asserts the slot, disables the controller and stops a running tree. Never a keyboard shortcut.
> viewer.html#enable
> commanding.html#viewer-slot

## Motion panel
aka: pose gizmo, Dive in place
part: ops
The viewer panel for moving Talos by hand: typed targets, a draggable pose gizmo, Dive in place, or one step per key press.
> viewer.html#motion

## Prior map editor
aka: Edit map
part: ops
The viewer's editor (Ctrl+M) for mapping's prior map: move props, type poses, save back to `config.yaml` on that laptop.
> viewer.html#mapping

## Scorecard
aka: task events
part: ops
In sim, the viewer scores a run by the 2026 rules from Nereus's task events, after Start run is pressed.
> nereus.html#tasks
> robosub.html#scoring

## Pool test
part: ops
A session at the pool with Talos: bench-test and freeze the code before, set up on deck, fly, record everything, then write it up.
> pool-test.html#shape

## Code freeze
aka: Pre RPAC test
part: ops
Before a pool test: commit each changed submodule, commit the new pins to the release repo ("Pre RPAC test …") and deploy exactly that.
> pool-test.html#freeze
> code-map.html#pinning

## Operator
aka: kill holder
part: ops
At the pool the operator is the only person at the viewer; a separate kill holder holds the physical switch and pulls it when anyone says so.
> pool-test.html#roles
> pool-test.html#kill

## Launcher log
aka: journal, journalctl
part: ops
A launched node's output goes only to the launcher page. The journal (`journalctl -u ros_launcher`) holds the launcher's own lines and the Zenoh router's crashes; each node's ROS log is in `~/.ros/log/` on the Orin.
> launch.html#logs
> debugging.html#logs

## Plots
aka: Topics window
part: ops
The viewer's live plots of any topic field, from its Windows menu.
> debugging.html#plots
> viewer.html#layouts

## Bench
aka: bench test, pretest bench
part: ops
Talos out of the water, on a table and a power supply: where new code is tried on the real Orin and the pre-test bench checks run. Never start the controller as a plain launch there.
> pool-test.html#pretest
