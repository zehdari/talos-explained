Autonomy · Chapter 3

The competition mission

One behaviour tree runs Talos's whole RoboSub run. This chapter reads it task by task, and lets you open every node of the real file while a Nereus run replays on top of it.

The short version
  • One file, RepairCompMpcTree.xml, runs the whole RoboSub run: kill switch, dive, gate, slalom, the pinger, torpedoes and table (the pinger picks which first), bins, magnets, then home through the gate.
  • A task that fails or runs out of time never stops the run: Talos moves on to the next one.
  • Each task has the same shape: tell mapping what to look for, search until its estimate is tight, lock the map, then move and act on that frozen estimate.
  • Between tasks Talos flies one path through waypoints fixed before the run.
  • In Nereus the run takes 8½ minutes from the kill switch, more than 3 of them at the table.

Chapter 1 explained behaviour trees and Chapter 2 the nodes Talos has. This is the tree that uses them: trees/RepairCompMpcTree.xml in riptide_autonomy (branch autonomy-mapping). The perception side of the same tasks is in Frames, targets and the mission tree.

The mission in one picture

How the file is built

  • A library plus a mission. The file is about 4,100 lines with 87 subtree definitions, because Groot saves the team's whole subtree library into every tree file. The mission uses 42 of them.
  • Repair or rescue. The main tree runs the mission with repair_mission = 1. RescueCompMpcTree.xml is the same file with 0, which picks the other side of the gate, the other torpedo holes and bin pictures, and other images at the table.
  • Who runs it. doTask ticks the tree every 30 ms until it returns SUCCESS or FAILURE, then turns the controller off and sets the LEDs (DoTask.cpp). You start it from the autonomy panel of the Nereus viewer, in the sim and on the robot alike.

Explore the real tree

The explorer below is the XML itself, parsed node for node, replayed on the same Nereus run: doTask publishes the names of the running nodes on autonomy/tree_stack, and that topic was recorded. Tap a task to jump there, ▸ to open a node, a row to see what it does. Try searching FollowPath for every move the mission makes, or 0.0005 for every place it waits for a 2 cm estimate.

Each task section below ends with a link that replays it in the explorer, and its "In Nereus" line gives times on the explorer's clock.

Reading the numbers

Every "until it is mapped" check compares mapping's error for the object with a threshold. The error comes from the estimate's covariance: it is in m², not metres. With the same spread σ in x, y and z it is √3·σ², so 0.0005 means σ ≈ 1.7 cm, 0.0025 ≈ 4 cm, 0.02 ≈ 11 cm and 0.3 ≈ 42 cm. The σ values on this page are worked out that way (MapConverged; Tree nodes that talk to mapping).

Before the gate

Nothing moves until the kill switch is in, and nothing the cameras see moves the map until a task asks for it.

Kill switch
LEDs solid red. Wait 5 s, then read the switch (state/kill) until it says not killed, and wait 1 s more. Then a blue flash and 5 s more (WaitForKillSwitch, XML). It runs once, at the start.
Prep
Lock the map with no target, reload the actuators and disarm them (PrepForRun).
Dive
Hold the heading and go down. The tree asks for −0.56 m, but the move (PrimitiveMovePosition) never goes shallower than −0.75 m. Within 25 cm counts as there, with 60 s to get there. Then level out (15 s).
If it fails
The dive is the one step that isn't wrapped: if Talos isn't at depth after 60 s, the whole mission ends with FAILURE.
In Nereus
Kill switch in at 0:33, dive done at 0:41 at about −0.52 m, which is within 25 cm of −0.75 m. Nereus starts Talos at about −0.33 m, so the dive took 2 s.

Gate

GateTreeMpc flies the gate where the prior map puts it. The map is still locked, so a detection can't move the gate; the gate's own search is switched off in the XML (an AlwaysFailure named "Comment out following").

Side
Repair goes through the repair half of the gate (gate_repair). Where the rescue half sits decides the slalom side: to the left means pass the slalom on the left.
Moves
One path (FollowPath): 1 m in front of the gate's frame, then through it, 1.1 m below the frame (−1.35 m in Nereus), 60 s. After the flip, 1 m out the back (30 s).
Flip
The flip (FlipTree) dates from before the MPC. It turns the controller off, tries to set an old controller parameter the MPC doesn't have, and gives up after 10 s. The MPC version (FlipTreeMpc) is in the file but never called, and untested in the mission.
In Nereus
0:41 to 1:04. During the 10 s with the thrusters off, Talos floated up from −1.35 m to −1.03 m.

Slalom

SlalomPathMpc looks once, locks the map, and flies the three pole sets as one path.

Look
Target the slalom as a whole (slalom_parent, the name the detector gives the pole sets). Park the forward camera 2.5 m in front, wait 5 s, then check the error every second until it is under 0.3 (σ ≈ 42 cm) or 15 s pass. Then lock the map.
Moves
One path (180 s). For the front, middle and back sets: 0.75 m before, level with it, 0.375 m after, all 0.8 m to the side (a quarter of the 3.2 m row) on the side the gate chose. Then on to the torpedo's waiting spot, holding the slalom's heading.
Why one path
The MPC flies through waypoints without stopping and settles only on the last. Each waypoint stays in its pole set's frame, so the path moves if mapping moves the frames.
In Nereus
1:04 to 1:39. The first check already read 0.00018.

The pinger picks the order

RandomPingerTree listens at two spots and decides whether the torpedo or the table comes first.

Listen
Lock the map. Go to the torpedo's waiting spot and record the hydrophone for 10 s; go to the table's waiting spot and record 10 s more. These two moves use the older point-to-point Move subtree, not a path.
Decide
riptide_acoustics compares how loud the pinger was in the two recordings, and answers true when the first stop (the torpedo's) was louder. The tree reads that answer as pinger_is_table. True: table, then torpedo. False: torpedo, then table.
If it fails
If a recording call fails, the answer is never read, and an unread answer counts as 0: torpedo first. If no answer comes within 3 s, it counts as false: torpedo first too.
In Nereus
1:39 to 2:12. The sim has no pinger, so the second recording fails to stop ("Cannot compare, buffer 0 has 0 samples"), its two retries get "Not sampling", the answer is never read, and the run goes torpedo first.

Between tasks

Talos gets from one task to the next on a single path through fixed waypoints. Nothing is searched on the way (CompMissionTreeMpc).

The waypoints
A waiting spot near each task and one on the way home (pre_torpedo_pose and three more), set before the run in mapping's config. Mapping publishes each as a frame. The spots "above the table" and "above the bins" hang 0.5 m over the table's and bins' waiting spots.
Do they move?
No detector sees them, but they are placed relative to where mapping thinks the course sits, so they shift with it (Frames, targets and the mission tree).
Depth
Every transit stays between 0.75 m and 2.5 m deep.
Torpedo first
  1. Target the torpedo board (map unlocked), then to its waiting spot, facing the board, 60 s.
  2. After the torpedo: torpedo spot → table spot → above the table, holding the torpedo heading, 120 s.
  3. After the table: above the table → table spot → bins spot → above the bins, holding the current heading, 120 s.
Table first
  1. Table spot → above the table, holding the current heading, 120 s.
  2. After the table: target the torpedo board (map unlocked), then above the table → table spot → torpedo spot, facing the board on the last leg, 120 s.
  3. After the torpedo: torpedo spot → bins spot → above the bins, holding the torpedo heading, 120 s.
If it fails
Each transit is wrapped on its own: if the path times out, the next task starts from wherever Talos stopped.
In Nereus
To the torpedo 2:12 to 2:24, to the table 2:45 to 3:02, to the bins 6:18 to 6:35.

Torpedoes

TorpedoTreeMpc maps the board on the way, searches until it is tight, then maps both holes from a spot in front of the board and shoots them one after the other.

On the way
Target the board (map unlocked), and fly to its waiting spot facing it (60 s).
Search
Forward camera. Sweep round the board (SearchMpc, below) until its error is under 0.0025 (σ ≈ 4 cm) or 100 s pass. Even if it times out, Talos shoots.
Holes
Arm. Hold a spot 1.5 m in front of the board, target the large and small holes together, wait up to 30 s until both are under 0.0005 (σ ≈ 1.7 cm), and lock the map.
Shot 1
Bring the first launcher (torpedo_0_link) 1.1 m, then 0.6 m from the large hole as one path, wait for the controller to settle, fire. Path and wait get two tries, each up to 60 s of path and 60 s of settling, so a shot can come up to about 4 minutes late, but it always comes.
Shot 2
Slide the second launcher to 0.6 m from the small hole, wait (two tries again), fire. Disarm.
Settled means
WaitForController: the MPC's trust is above 0.985 on every axis, and the launcher's sideways (y) offset in the hole's frame is under 1 cm, within 60 s. Trust rises toward 1 while Talos sits within 2 cm and 2° of its setpoint and nearly still (settle_trust.hpp).
In Nereus
2:24 to 2:45. The board was centred 2.8 s after the search began; both holes converged 1.5 s after being targeted.

Table

NewTableTreeMpc maps the two baskets, fits the table to them, then sorts four items into the baskets with the claw.

Setup
Down camera. All six table props (helmet, warning, bandage, pill, nut and bolt, plug) become background targets: mapping updates each one whenever it sees it, whatever the current target.
Baskets
Search the helmet basket, then the warning basket, with the down camera 1.5 m above (DownwardSearchMpc), to 0.0005 each, 60 s. If both pass in one 1 s check, mapping moves the table onto them; otherwise the table keeps its prior.
Four sorts
Bandage and pill into the helmet basket, nut and bolt and plug into the warning basket. Each is a pickup, a surface and a drop.
Pickup
Search the item to 0.5 (just "seen"), 180 s. Hover the camera 1.5 m above it for up to 5 s until 0.0005. Lock the map, open the claw, bring the claw 2 m above, then 15 cm, then onto the item (22 mm higher for the shorter bandage and plug), waiting for the controller (trust 0.95, 1 cm) at the last two. Close, and 2 s later back up.
Surface
Over the table's centre, command a depth of −0.3 m until above −0.35 m (15 s), then back down.
Drop
Claw over the basket at its current height, then 2 m above it, then 15 cm. Wait for the controller, lock, open for 0.3 s, back up to 2 m, close.
Finish
Go over the midpoint of the baskets (the tree asks for 1.5 m above it; the move keeps to the −0.75 m minimum depth, as in the dive), face the hammer-and-wrench image (rescue: sos), spin one full turn per item sorted, face it again and surface. Disarm, clear the background targets, switch to the forward camera.
Claw moves
The claw and surface moves use the older point-to-point Move subtree, not a path.
In Nereus
3:02 to 6:18, all four items. The table moved 5 cm and turned 0.6° to fit the baskets. Each sort took about 34 s.

Bins

The bin has two identical fire pictures. The binary classifier tells them apart as two targets, and BinTreeMpc drops one marker on each.

Start
Down camera. Start the classifier on fire (rescue: blood), 3 tries. If it won't start, the bins are skipped.
Target 1
Search to 0.02 (σ ≈ 11 cm), 120 s, camera 2.15 m above. Arm.
Drop 1
Hover the camera over it for up to 3 s until 0.0005, freeze the classifier's buffer, lock, put the dropper 1 m above, wait for the controller (trust 0.90, 1 cm, 30 s), drop. It checks a marker is left first (ShootDownwardObjectMpc).
Target 2
Switch the classifier to the second picture, then the same search and drop. Stop the classifier, disarm.
Freeze refused
The freeze before the second drop is refused, since mapping only freezes while tracking the first picture. The call is wrapped, so the drop goes ahead on the unfrozen estimate.
One difference
If the second target isn't under 0.02 after 120 s, the second marker is never dropped. The first drop happens whatever its search found.
In Nereus
6:35 to 7:07. The second target needed the spiral: 8 s to come into view.

Magnets

MagnetTreeMpc runs inside the bins task, right after the drops, and uses them.

Fit
Mapping fits the bin's CAD model to the two classified pictures, which places the magnets where the CAD puts them (bin fit).
Classify
Start the classifier on the magnets, which gives two targets.
Look
Forward camera. Path to 1 m off magnet 1, facing it, at −0.8 m. A quick search (MiniSearchMpc) to 1.25, a loose "seen" check, 20 s.
Touch
Freeze the buffer, lock. Bring the magnet tool (magnet_link) to 0.7 m in front of the magnet, turned and at depth in one move, then straight in; wait for the controller (trust 0.8, 1 cm, 20 s); hold 2 s.
Magnet 2
Back out and on to magnet 2's search spot in one path, then the same. Finally back out again.

How Talos searches

Three search subtrees do most of the looking. All of them end when mapping's error for the object drops below the threshold, or when time runs out.

Quick look
MiniSearchMpc. Slalom, magnets. Park the forward camera in front of the object, set the target, wait 5 s, then check every second. Once the error is under 0.1 it re-centres in front of the new estimate.
Sweep
SearchMpc. Torpedo board. Sweep arcs round the estimate while swinging the heading. As soon as the object is seen, chase it square on and hold it in view up to 8 s. A sweep that saw nothing makes the next one wider.
Down search
DownwardSearchMpc. Baskets, items, bins. Skip if already mapped well enough. Fly the down camera over the estimate at full speed; once the object is in view, re-aim at its latest detection every 0.5 s. Otherwise spiral out at 0.2 m/s.

When something fails

What goes wrongWhat the tree does
A whole task failsIts wrapper (ForceSuccess) turns the failure into a success; the next task starts.
A search times outSearches are wrapped too: Talos shoots, grabs or drops on the estimate it has. One exception: the second bin target, whose drop is skipped.
The controller won't settleEvery settle wait before a shot, grab or touch is wrapped: Talos acts anyway.
A path times out, or makes no progress for 10 sThe rest of that sequence is skipped. A gate approach that times out skips the flip and the exit.
An actuator call failsArming, torpedoes and droppers all sit inside a wrapper named "REMOVE ME": they can't fail the tree, so nothing retries them.
The table's last move failsIts last two moves aren't wrapped. If one fails, the rest of the table task is skipped: Talos stays armed, the background targets are never cleared, and the forward camera isn't switched back before the bins.
A camera switch failsThe sequence it starts is skipped: the torpedo search, the bins, or that one table item.
A classifier won't startThe bins (or the magnets) are skipped.
The dive doesn't reach depthThe mission ends with FAILURE after 60 s.
The kill switch is pulledThe thrusters stop, but the tree keeps running (below).
The kill switch does not stop the mission
  • The tree keeps running. doTask's kill check is commented out (DoTask.cpp), and the wait for the switch only runs at the start.
  • It hurries on while killed. The MPC cuts thrust and aborts the path it was flying ("vehicle killed"), and aborts every new path the same way (mpc_controller_node.cpp). Settle waits run out their time with trust at 0, and the wrappers move the mission on from task to task.
  • Putting the switch back in resumes the run at whatever step the tree has reached, possibly several tasks later, and Talos flies whatever the tree commands next.
  • To end a run, stop the tree: Stop in the viewer's Autonomy panel, or KILL in its command bar (Running a tree). That needs the tether; untethered, keep the switch out until you can stop the tree.

What the LEDs say

In the pool the LEDs are often the only sign of what the tree is doing. The tree sets them with SetStatus (source); doTask sets the last one when the tree ends (DoTask.cpp).

LEDsMeaning
Solid redWaiting for the kill switch (waiting).
Slow blue flashSwitch in, 5 s to go (starting).
Solid orangeA search is running (searching).
Slow magenta flashMoving into place for a task's action (aligning).
Fast blue flashDoing it: the gate pass, a shot, a drop, the claw, a magnet touch (performing).
Breathing greenThe tree ended with SUCCESS.
Breathing redThe tree ended with FAILURE, or was stopped.
Fast red flashAn error while ticking the tree aborted it (DoTask.cpp).

The mission never sets solid green (moving); only the old buoy task does. Between tasks the LEDs keep whatever the last task left.

Things to watch

  • The controller goes off for 10 s in the gate. The old flip turns it off (Gate). Drop the flip, or switch to FlipTreeMpc once it has been tested.
  • The pinger's answer may be backwards. A louder torpedo stop means table first, but points go for scoring first at the task the active pinger marks (RoboSub and the course), so it should mean torpedo first. Settle which is right before a competition.
  • In max mode the pinger ignores a clear winner. The launch file starts riptide_acoustics in max mode. There, compare_max_buffers only answers when the two loudest samples are within 1000 of each other (it then compares averages). When one stop is clearly louder it keeps its last answer, false at start: torpedo first (Acoustics.cpp). Launch it with mode:=avg, or fix compare_max_buffers.
  • The second torpedo doesn't wait for the first. FireTorpedo asks for a 10 s wait through a key ("10") it never sets, so the wait gives up at once ("Actuators busy!") and the second shot follows about 6 s after the first. Set the key, as FireDropper does.
  • Empty ports read as zero. A port mapped to a key nobody set reads as empty, and most nodes then use a default. This run's log warns about four, among them the pinger's comparison and the torpedo wait above. After every run, read the "does not have a value for required port" warnings.
  • Drops skip their approach. The claw's approach move sits in a retry with a 0 s timeout, which fails before running it, so the claw goes straight to the basket's current estimate (dropObjectMpc). Give the retry a real timeout.
  • Settling checks only the sideways offset. A tool at the right side offset but the wrong height or range still counts as settled (WaitForController). Read out_x and out_z from the same TransformPose and check them too before firing.
  • Nereus refused both second releases in this run with "Release group is busy"; the cause is unknown (How the trees use the actuators).
  • Numbers stored under their own name. Each subtree has its own blackboard, and a SubTree attribute names a key of the caller, never a value; to pass 1, the caller first stores 1 under the key "1". Keep these when tidying a tree.
  • One dead block. Right after the pinger, a block sets target_image, but no subtree is passed that key; the table picks its own image.
  • Perception side. The one-second basket check, pickups that may not refine, and a bins classifier that can block the magnets are in Frames, targets and the mission tree.
